How to open OpenMount

The first time you open it, macOS will block OpenMount. That's normal. Follow these steps once (about a minute) and it opens like any other app from then on.

Why does this happen? OpenMount is a free, open-source app made by a volunteer. Apple only lets apps skip this check if the developer pays for a yearly Apple developer account, which OpenMount doesn't have. The full source code is public on GitHub, so anyone can check what it does.

  1. Drag OpenMount into Applications

    In the OpenMount window (the one this guide came from), drag the OpenMount icon onto the Applications folder.

  2. Open it and click Done

    Open Applications and double-click OpenMount. macOS will say it was “Not Opened”.

    ⚠️ Click Done. Don't click the blue Move to Trash button, even though it's highlighted.

  3. Go to Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway

    Open System Settings, then click Privacy & Security in the sidebar and scroll down to the Security section. Next to “OpenMount.app was blocked to protect your Mac”, click Open Anyway.

    💡 The Open Anyway button only appears for about an hour after step 2. If you don't see it, open OpenMount again and repeat step 2.

  4. Confirm with Open Anyway, then Touch ID or your password

    macOS asks one more time. Click Open Anyway (the middle button), then confirm with Touch ID or your Mac's password.

    ⚠️ Again, Move to Trash is the highlighted button. Pick Open Anyway.

  5. ✓ That's it, OpenMount is open

    Plug in your Light Mount and it connects automatically. You won't see these warnings again for this version.

Prefer Terminal? One-line alternative

After dragging OpenMount into Applications, run this once in Terminal. It removes the “downloaded from the internet” flag from OpenMount only:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/OpenMount.app

What about updates?

OpenMount tells you when a new version is out. Click Update now and it updates itself, with no need to repeat these steps. Your keyboard settings live on the keyboard itself and aren't affected.
